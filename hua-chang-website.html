About

Scholar, Educator, Program Leader

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing and Program Director of the Master's in Marketing Intelligence program at Towson University's College of Business & Economics. I also serve as an AI Research Fellow, exploring how artificial intelligence transforms marketing strategy and consumer engagement.

My research focuses on brand management, digital and social media marketing, and consumer behavior. I have published in leading academic outlets including the Journal of Business Ethics, Journal of Business Research, International Journal of Advertising, European Journal of Marketing, and Journal of Product & Brand Management.

Prior to joining Towson, I held a faculty position at Philadelphia University and completed my Ph.D. in Marketing at Drexel University's LeBow College of Business. I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in Digital Marketing and Marketing Principles, consistently earning high student evaluations.

Research

Publications & Scholarship

My research investigates how brands connect with consumers in digital environments, with a focus on brand strategy, AI-driven personalization, and ethical consumption.

◆

Brand Management

Brand positioning, brand ownership, artist-brand alliances, cross-border brand acquisitions, and brand moral failures.

◈

Digital & Social Media Marketing

AI personalization, online product customization, consumer-brand relationships on social media, and digital consumer engagement.

◇

Consumer Behavior

Construal level theory, psychological ownership, ethical judgment, green advertising, and the role of authenticity in consumption.
