Research

Publications & Scholarship

My research investigates how brands connect with consumers in digital environments, with a focus on brand strategy, AI-driven personalization, and ethical consumption.

Focus

Research Areas

◆

Brand Management

Brand positioning and ownership, artist-brand alliances, cross-border brand acquisitions, brand moral failures, and customization.

◈

Digital & AI Marketing

Human-AI collaboration in customer service, AI-driven personalization, consumer responses to AI-generated content, and social media engagement.

◇

Consumer Behavior

Construal level, psychological ownership, ethical judgment, green advertising, and perceived authenticity.

Towson AI Institute

Faculty Lead: Human-AI Interaction

As a Faculty Lead at the Towson AI Institute (TAI), I build cross-disciplinary research collaborations on human-AI interaction in consumer and organizational contexts, connecting researchers in marketing, computer science, and information systems.

⚙

Human-AI Service

Experiments on how consumers evaluate and trust human-only, AI-only, and hybrid human-AI customer service.

✦

Authenticity in AI Advertising

How consumers judge the authenticity of advertising created with generative AI.

◎

AI-Personalized Communication

How AI-personalized brand messages on social media shape consumer engagement.

TAI Initiatives

Cross-disciplinary funding proposals on applied AI and consumer behavior.

Co-Authored Empirical Research

Collaborative studies of human-AI interaction across colleges.

Cross-College Workshop Series
